About Us

About King Health

King Health is an independent insurance advisory built on the belief that every family deserves protection chosen with care, explained with patience and priced within reach.

Our mission

At King Health, we care for the people we serve the way they care for the people they love.

Every family deserves the quiet confidence of knowing that those they cherish will be provided for, and we hold that trust as a privilege rather than a transaction. We listen before we recommend, we compare with diligence so that protection fits the budget as faithfully as it fits the need, and we remain beside our clients long after the paperwork is signed. Our purpose is to make the coverage every household deserves understandable, attainable and lasting, so that each family can meet the future with dignity, provision and peace of mind.

Founder and principal advisor

The story behind King Health

Javon King founded King Health because he saw how often families made important coverage decisions without anyone taking the time to explain their choices or to look for an option they could comfortably afford. He built the advisory to offer something better: patient, personal counsel from someone who cares about a client's loved ones as much as the client does.

Today Javon leads King Health as its founder and principal advisor. He and our team work with seniors navigating Medicare, families protecting their futures, employers caring for their teams and retirees seeking dependable income. In every conversation, the commitment is the same: to understand what matters most to you, to compare your options with care and to recommend only what serves you well.
